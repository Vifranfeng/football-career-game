The '''2025 K3 League''' is the sixth season of the semi-professional [[K3 League]], the third-highest league of men's [[football in South Korea]].

On 11 August 2025, [[Gimhae FC 2008]] and [[Paju Citizen FC|Paju Citizen]] were approved to join the [[K League 2]] for the [[2026 K League 2|2026 season]] after switching to professional teams at the fourth meeting of the K League Federation. This season was their last season in semi-professional leagues.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20250811123100007 |script-title=ko:김해·용인·파주 회원가입 승인…내년 K리그2 17개 팀으로 |lang=ko |publisher=Yonhap News Agency |date=2025-08-11 |access-date=2025-12-23}}</ref>

== Competition format ==
The pre-season winter transfer window opened between 1 January 2025 and 24 March 2025, and the summer transfer window opened between 20 June 2025 and 31 July 2025.{{Citation needed |date=January 2025}}

The 2025 season was held from 1 March to 8 November, and consisted of 30 rounds, with each team playing 28 matches.<ref>{{Cite Instagram |postid=DFpTGfpSx1E |user=thekfa_k3k4league |script-title=ko:2025 K4리그 일정표 |author=Korea Football Association |date=February 5, 2025 |access-date=February 6, 2025 |language=ko |trans-title=2025 K4 League schedule |link=https://www.instagram.com/p/DFpTGfpSx1E/?img_index=7}}</ref><ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Ko |first=Sung-hwan |date=27 February 2025 |script-title=ko:'15개팀 참가' 2025 K3리그, 3월 1일 개막...8개월 대장정 막 올린다 |trans-title=With 15 participating teams, the 2025 K3 League will begin its 8-month odyssey on 1 March |url=https://www.chosun.com/sports/football/2025/02/27/MIMTWAPALMY3Y47FRGV2BS35T4/ |access-date=28 February 2025 |website=The Chosun Ilbo |language=ko}}</ref> One team took a rest in each round as the number of participating teams was an odd number.<ref name=":0" />

While fifteen teams competed in the 2025 season, each of them had to include at least three under-23 players in the matchday squad, and at least one of those players had to include in the starting line-up.<ref name=":1">{{Cite web |last=Lee |first=Young-ho |date=27 February 2025 |script-title=ko:K3리그, 2025시즌 3월 1일 개막... 15개팀 경쟁 |trans-title=The 2025 season of the K3 League will be started on 1 March, with 15 teams competing |url=https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20250227149500007?section=sports/all |access-date=28 February 2025 |website=Yonhap News Agency |language=ko}}</ref>

The 15th-placed team advanced to the [[#Relegation play-off|relegation play-off]], where they played runners-up of the [[2025 K4 League]].<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.munhwa.com/article/11531511 |script-title=ko:대한축구협회, 2025시즌 K3·K4리그 승강 팀 수·방식 변경 |lang=ko |publisher=Munhwa Ilbo |date=2025-09-08 |access-date=2025-12-24}}</ref>

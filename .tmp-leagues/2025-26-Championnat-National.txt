The '''2025–26 Championnat National''' season is the 33rd since the establishment of the [[Championnat National]] and the 27th in its current format, which serves as third division of the [[French football league system]].

This season is also be the last to use the National name, with the league being renamed [[Ligue 3]] starting in the 2026–27 season.<ref>{{cite web|last=|first=|date=17 January 2025|url=https://lequipe.fr/Football/Actualites/La-fff-veut-creer-une-ligue-3-professionnelle-en-2026/1532766/ |title=La FFF confirme son projet d'une Ligue 3 professionnelle en 2026 |language=fr |website=L'Equipe |access-date=22 July 2025}}</ref>

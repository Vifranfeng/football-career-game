The '''2026–27 EFL League One''', referred to as the '''[[Sky Betting & Gaming|Sky Bet]] League One''' due to sponsorship reasons, will be the 23rd season of the [[EFL League One]] under its current title, and the 35th season under its current league division format. The season will start between 14 and 16 August 2026, and end in late April/early May 2027.

== Summary ==

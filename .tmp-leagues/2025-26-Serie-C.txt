The '''2025–26 Serie C''', officially known as '''Serie C [[Sky Italia|Sky Wifi]]''' for sponsorship,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.skygroup.sky/it-it/article/sky-wifi-sponsor-serie-c-stagione-2025-26|title=Sky Wifi nuovo title sponsor della Serie C per la stagione 2025-26|date=17 June 2025}}</ref> was the twelfth season of the unified [[Serie C]] division, the third tier of the [[Italian football league system]].

==Changes==
The league will be composed of 60 teams, geographically divided into three groups. The group composition will be decided and formalized by the Serie C league committee between July and August. Inter Milan have shown interest in adding their U23 team, but the change requires the approval of the league and an available spot, whereas [[Lucchese 1905|Lucchese]] opted not to apply for a league spot instead.<ref>{{Cite web |date=24 February 2025|title=Marotta: "L'Inter investirà sui giovani". Pronta la seconda squadra Under 23 |url=https://www.lastampa.it/sport/2025/02/24/news/inter_marotta_assemblea-15021155/ |access-date=13 May 2025|website=La Stampa |language=it}}</ref>

According to the Italian Football Federation regulations, retired and folded clubs are included and counted among the ordinary seasonal relegations, while excluded clubs must be replaced, in order:

* by a Serie A [[reserve team]];
* by a [[Serie D]] playoff winner.<ref>[https://figc.it/media/259408/172-ordine-integrazione-organico-serie-c-2025-2026.pdf]</ref>

Between June and July 2025, [[Brescia Calcio|Brescia]] and [[SPAL]]'s applications were rejected by the COVISOC (the Italian Football Federation's office responsible for evaluating the financial state of professional clubs).<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.figc.it/it/federazione/news/spal-esclusa-dalla-serie-c-deliberata-la-mancata-concessione-della-licenza-nazionale/ |publisher=FIGC |language=it |date=19 June 2025 |accessdate=18 July 2025 |title=SPAL esclusa dalla Serie C: deliberata la mancata concessione della Licenza Nazionale}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.calcioefinanza.it/2025/07/03/esclusione-brescia-serie-c-motivazioni-covisoc/ |publisher=Calcio e Finanza |language=it |date=3 July 2025 |accessdate=18 July 2025 |title=Dai debiti ai mancati pagamenti: perché la Covisoc non ha ammesso il Brescia}}</ref> As a consequence, it was widely expected [[Inter Milan]]'s Under-23 team and [[Ravenna FC|Ravenna]] to be admitted to the league.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.ravennaedintorni.it/sport/2025/07/14/calcio-ravenna-serie-c/ |publisher=Ravenna e dintorni |language=it |date=14 July 2025 |title=Il Ravenna che aspetta la C: ufficialità il 24 luglio, gironi il 25, campionato al via il 24 agosto (in diretta su Sky e Now)}}</ref>

Also, on 17 July 2025, [[Feralpisalò]] chairman and owner Giuseppe Pasini formally confirmed his club would rename to [[Union Brescia]] and relocate to the city of [[Brescia]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.rainews.it/tgr/lombardia/articoli/2025/07/calcio-nasce-ufficialmente-lunion-brescia-la-presentazione-a-palazzo-loggia-89df58f3-3520-44a7-9d66-6d4307f55c68.html |publisher=RaiNews |language=it |date=17 July 2025 |accessdate=18 July 2025 |title=Calcio, nasce ufficialmente l’Union Brescia: la presentazione a palazzo Loggia}}</ref>

On 24 July 2025, the Serie C Federal Council ratified the exclusion of [[Lucchese 1905|Lucchese]], [[SPAL]], and [[Brescia Calcio|Brescia]], approved the reinstatement of [[Aurora Pro Patria 1919|Pro Patria]], and confirmed the admission of [[Inter Milan Under-23]] and [[Ravenna FC|Ravenna]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.corrieredellosport.it/news/calcio/2025/07/24-142162941/inter_u23_ravenna_e_pro_patria_in_serie_c_ecco_le_escluse_dalla_figc |publisher=Corriere dello Sport |language=it |date=24 July 2025 |accessdate=24 July 2025 |title=Inter U23, Ravenna e Pro Patria in Serie C. Ecco le escluse dalla Figc}}</ref>

On 28 November 2025, the Italian Football Federation formally excluded [[Rimini FC 1912|Rimini]] from the league with immediate effect following the club's liquidation.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.figc.it/it/federazione/news/la-figc-revoca-laffiliazione-al-rimini-football-club-decadono-tutti-i-tesseramenti-u09gpr1s |publisher=FIGC |language=it |date=28 November 2025 |accessdate=28 November 2025 |title=La FIGC revoca l’affiliazione al Rimini Football Club, decadono tutti i tesseramenti}}</ref>

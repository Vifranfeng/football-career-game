The '''2026–27 Primera Federación''' season is the sixth for the [[Primera Federación]], the third-highest level in the [[Spanish football league system]]. Forty teams will participate, divided into two groups of twenty clubs each based on geographical proximity. In each group, the champions are automatically promoted to [[Segunda División]] and the second to fifth placers will play promotion play-offs and the bottom five are relegated to the [[Segunda Federación]].

==Overview before the season==
A total of 40 teams joined the league, including four relegated from the [[2025–26 Segunda División]], 26 retained from the [[2025–26 Primera Federación]], and ten promoted from the [[2025–26 Segunda Federación]]. The groups were defined on 24 June 2026, keeping the same criteria from the last season.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://rfef.es/es/noticias/aprobados-los-grupos-de-primera-federacion-para-la-temporada-202627|title=Aprobados los grupos de Primera Federación para la temporada 2026/27|trans-title=Approved the Primera Federación groups for the 2026/27 season|publisher=[[Royal Spanish Football Federation]]|language=es|date=24 June 2026|accessdate=24 June 2026}}</ref>

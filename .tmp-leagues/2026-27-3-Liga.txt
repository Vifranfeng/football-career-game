The '''2026–27 3. Liga''' will be the 19th season of the [[3. Liga]]. It will start on 7 August 2026 and will conclude on 22 May 2027.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.dfb.de/news/rahmenterminkalender-fuer-saison-2026/2027-festgelegt|title=Rahmenterminkalender für Saison 2026/2027 festgelegt|website=kicker.de |publisher=[[Kicker (magazine)|kicker]] |date=6 November 2025 |access-date=6 November 2025 |language=de}}</ref>

The '''2025 China Resources Beverage Chinese Football League 2''' ({{lang-zh|2025华润饮料中国足球乙级联赛}}) was the 36th season of [[China League Two]], the third tier of the [[Chinese football league system|Chinese football league pyramid]], since its establishment in 1989.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dongqiudi.com/articles/4926977.html|title=官方：华润饮料冠名新赛季中国三级联赛|publisher=Dongqiudi|date=22 February 2025|access-date=1 October 2025|language=zh}}</ref>

===Name changes===
*Guangzhou Alpha F.C. changed their name to [[Guangzhou Dandelion Alpha F.C.|Guangzhou Dandelion Alpha]] in December 2024.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.thecfa.cn/qttz/20241227/35440.html|title=中国足球协会关于安华足球俱乐部（广州）有限公司更名的公告|publisher=Chinese FA|date=27 December 2024|access-date=7 January 2025|language=zh}}</ref>
*Xi'an Chongde Ronghai F.C.'s name change to [[Yan'an Ronghai F.C.|Yan'an Ronghai]] was put into effect in January 2025.
*Rizhao Yuqi F.C. relocated to [[Lanzhou]], [[Gansu]], and changed their name to [[Lanzhou Longyuan Athletic F.C.|Lanzhou Longyuan Athletic]] in February 2025.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.thecfa.cn/qttz/20250220/35648.html|title=中国足球协会关于日照宇启足球俱乐部有限公司更名的公告|publisher=Chinese Football Association|date=20 February 2025|access-date=21 February 2025|language=zh}}</ref>
*Langfang Glory City F.C. relocated to [[Hangzhou]], [[Zhejiang]], and changed their name to [[Hangzhou Linping Wuyue F.C.|Hangzhou Linping Wuyue]] in February 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |title=中国足球协会关于廊坊荣耀之城足球俱乐部有限公司更名的公告-中国足球协会官方网站 |url=https://www.thecfa.cn/qttz/20250220/35646.html |access-date=2025-03-02 |website=www.thecfa.cn}}</ref>
*Haikou Mingcheng F.C. relocated to [[Kunming]], [[Yunnan]], and changed their name to [[Kunming City F.C.|Kunming City]] in February 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |title=中国足球协会关于海口名城足球俱乐部有限公司更名的公告-中国足球协会官方网站 |url=https://www.thecfa.cn/qttz/20250220/35647.html |access-date=2025-03-02 |website=www.thecfa.cn}}</ref>
*Jiangxi Dark Horse Junior F.C. relocated to [[Wenzhou]], [[Zhejiang]], and changed their name to [[Wenzhou Professional F.C.|Wenzhou]] in February 2025.<ref>{{Cite web |title=中国足球协会关于江西黑马青年足球俱乐部有限公司更名的公告-中国足球协会官方网站 |url=https://www.thecfa.cn/qttz/20250220/35645.html |access-date=2025-03-02 |website=www.thecfa.cn}}</ref>
*Yan'an Ronghai F.C. relocated to [[Taiyuan]], [[Shanxi]], and changed their name to [[Shanxi Chongde Ronghai F.C.|Shanxi Chongde Ronghai]] in February 2025.
